# Insights Summary – Olist E-Commerce Analytics

**Dataset:** Olist Brazilian E-Commerce | **Tools:** PostgreSQL, Python, Power BI

## Business Snapshot

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | R$ 13,221,498 |
| Total Orders | 96,478 |
| Average Order Value | ~R$ 137 |
| Total Customers | 93,358 |
| Avg Delivery Time | 12.5 days |
| Avg Review Score | 4.09 / 5 |

## Key Insights

**1. Customer retention is the biggest weakness.**
Only 3.0% of customers (2,801 of 93,358) placed more than one order. Almost all revenue comes from one-time buyers, so the business depends heavily on constantly acquiring new customers.
*Recommendation:* Run post-purchase campaigns, loyalty offers and win-back emails to lift repeat purchases.

**2. Revenue is concentrated in a few states.**
São Paulo (SP) alone brings R$ 5.07M, about 38% of total revenue. SP, RJ and MG together account for about 63%, and the top 5 states for about 74%.
*Recommendation:* Protect the SP/RJ/MG market, and test targeted marketing in mid-sized states such as RS and PR to reduce dependence on three states.

**3. Revenue is spread across several categories, not one.**
The top 5 categories (health_beauty, watches_gifts, bed_bath_table, sports_leisure, computers_accessories) make up about 40% of revenue, with health_beauty leading at R$ 1.23M.
*Recommendation:* Prioritise stock and promotions for these categories, and use them for cross-selling.

**4. Delivery performance is good, but 1 in 12 orders is late.**
91.9% of orders arrive on time. 8.1% (7,826 orders) are late, and the average delivery takes 12.5 days.
*Recommendation:* Find which states and sellers cause most delays and improve logistics there. Faster delivery can also help repeat purchases.

**5. Customer satisfaction is healthy.**
The average review score is 4.09 out of 5, which suggests product quality and service are generally acceptable.
*Recommendation:* Analyse low-score reviews alongside late deliveries to check how much delay hurts ratings.

## Conclusion

The business sells well and customers are mostly satisfied, but growth is limited by very low repeat purchases and high dependence on a few states. Focusing on retention and regional expansion offers the clearest opportunity.
